# Seven Realms

A fantasy action RPG for the browser, inspired by Vampire Survivors. Pick a class, enter the gates, choose one of seven continents, and survive as long as you can while monsters swarm you from every side. Your weapon attacks on its own, so all you do is move, dodge, collect, and choose upgrades.

## Purpose

Seven Realms is a quick, replayable survival RPG. A run takes a few minutes, and everything you earn (gold, materials, shop upgrades) carries over, so each run makes the next one stronger. It runs from a single HTML file with no install and works on desktop and phone.

## Goal

- **In a run:** survive. Stay alive, collect XP gems, level up, and pick boons that make your hero stronger.
- **Bosses:** a boss arrives every 3 minutes, and a swarm of 22 monsters closes in every 75 seconds.
- **Quests:** complete the NPC quests in each continent for gold and XP.
- **Long term:** earn gold, spend it in the Shop on permanent upgrades, and beat your best survival time in all seven realms.

The game has no final ending. Your score is how long you last, how many monsters you slay, and how high you level.

## How the game flows

1. **Choose your Calling:** pick one of six classes.
2. **Choose your gender:** male or female.
3. **Name your hero:** up to 8 letters.
4. **The Gates:** two great doors slowly swing open.
5. **Main menu:** Play, Inventory, Shop, Settings.
6. **Play:** a world map with seven continents. Click one and your light travels there.
7. **Wake up:** your hero opens their eyes and the run begins.
8. **Run:** move, fight, quest, level up. Retreat or fall, and you return to the menu with your gold.

## Controls

- **Move:** WASD or arrow keys. On a phone, drag anywhere to use the touch stick.
- **Talk to an NPC:** walk up and press E, or tap the Talk button.
- **Health potion:** press Q, or tap the Potion button.
- **Pause:** press Esc or P, or tap the pause button.

## What is inside

- **Play:** the seven-continent map and the survival runs.
- **Inventory:** your hero, your five permanent upgrades, and the materials and potions you carry.
- **Shop:** buy health potions and permanent upgrades, and sell materials for gold.
- **Settings:** sound effects and music volume, damage numbers, screen shake, touch stick, and erasing your save.
- **Level-up boons:** each level you pick 1 of 3 random boons, such as Might (+15% damage), Haste (attack 10% faster), Reach (+20% range), Keen Eye (+8% crit), Swiftness (+10% move speed), Vitality (+25 max health), Recovery (regenerate health), Magnetism (+35% pickup range), Fortune (+20% gold), and Bulwark (+1 armor). Each class also gets its own multi-shot boon: Volley, Twin Blades, Arcane Split, Whirlwind, Aftershock, or Phalanx.
- **Permanent upgrades (Shop):** Whetstone (+10% damage per level), Iron Plate (+10% max health), Swift Boots (+4% move speed), Lucky Charm (+10% gold), and Magnet Ring (+20% pickup range). Each goes up to level 8.
- **Saving:** your hero, gold, upgrades, and best times are saved in your browser automatically.

## Quests

Every continent has three NPCs with a glowing "!" above their heads. Walk up and press E to talk. You can accept or decline.

- **Slay quest:** defeat 20 to 30 monsters. Reward: 70 gold and XP.
- **Gather quest:** collect 5 to 7 of the continent's material, dropped by monsters. Reward: 90 gold and XP.
- **Scout quest:** travel to a glowing marker on the map and return. Reward: 110 gold, XP, and a Health Potion.

After you accept, the "!" turns into a progress marker. When the quest is done, return to the NPC and claim your reward.

### Replies that every NPC uses

- **Quest offered** (buttons: Decline / Accept Quest): the NPC's line above, then the quest title and reward.
- **Quest in progress** (button: Keep Going): "Still at it? Good. I am counting on you."
- **Quest complete** (button: Claim Reward): "You did it. I knew you had it in you."
- **After the reward** (button: Farewell): "The realm owes you. Safe travels, *(hero name)*."

### Story and system messages

- Doors: "The gates of Seven Realms stir…" then "Welcome, *(hero name)*."
- Map: "Select a continent to send your light there." then "Your light descends…"
- Boss: "Boss: *(boss name)*" and, when it falls, "Boss defeated".
- Swarm: "A swarm approaches".
- Level up: "Level *N*. Choose a boon".
- Death: "You have fallen". The screen shows the realm, time survived, monsters slain, level reached, quests done, gold earned, and your best time.

## Tech

- One self-contained HTML file (HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, drawn on a canvas)
- No libraries, no downloads, no build step
- Sound effects and music are generated in the browser
- Progress is saved in the browser's local storage
